## [COMM3050 - Data Use and Misuse](https://www.handbook.unsw.edu.au/undergraduate/courses/2026/COMM3050)

An Undergraduate course on the uses and misuses of data, offered by the School of Management and Governance, UNSW Business School.

## [COMM5010 - Strategy, Marketing and Management](https://www.handbook.unsw.edu.au/postgraduate/courses/2026/COMM5010)

A Postgraduate course on different strategies, marketing and management concepts and methods, offered by the School of Marketing, UNSW Business School.

## [COMM5601 - Interdisiplinary Applications of Analytics Techniques](https://www.handbook.unsw.edu.au/postgraduate/courses/2026/COMM5601)

A Postgraduate course on the interdisiplinary applications of analytics techniques, offered by the UNSW Business School.
